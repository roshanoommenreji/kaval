- Pushed one commit to #421's branch: the value in both Dockerfiles; the live lines of Lab 80 and ADR-0079's toolchain row now name Go 1.27.4 and the new digest (dated measurement records stay as measured).
- The CI step that checks `opa version` reads the Go version from the Dockerfile, so no workflow change was needed.
