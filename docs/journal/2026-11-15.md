## Dependabot's base-image bump needed the new OPA fingerprint (PR #421)

#421 moved the Go compiler image from 1.27.3 to 1.27.4 (a security release, 12 fixes, 10 Nov) plus new python and uv digests. Two of its five image builds failed (agent and executor, the two that compile OPA): `opa checksum differs`. - Both failed builds, on two separate runners, printed the same value: `e96a03f6...563f0c1` (was `f6850f6c...228ba3`).
- Pushed one commit to #421's branch: the value in both Dockerfiles; the live lines of Lab 80 and ADR-0079's toolchain row now name Go 1.27.4 and the new digest (dated measurement records stay as measured).
- The CI step that checks `opa version` reads the Go version from the Dockerfile, so no workflow change was needed.
