## KAV-102: what the overnight run showed

The EKS lab was left running overnight (the live run stopped mid-way for the night), and the self-destruct timer did its job without help: servers to zero at 4 h 09 m, load balancer and server group deleted at 6 h 09 m, cluster at 6 h 24 m, target group at 6 h 39 m (CloudTrail and the timer's own log agree). Total spend about $1.60. The orphan database disk and the hand-made pull permission were removed by hand, `terraform destroy` removed the other 38 resources.

One thing for the sweep: it reads the label index (Resource Groups Tagging API), which lags behind a deletion, so it kept listing the already-deleted disk after `describe-volumes` said it was gone. A follow-up for `lab_sweep.py` is to confirm each tagged resource still exists before reporting it.
