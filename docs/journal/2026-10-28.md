## A new idea recorded as future scope: Petti, a box for the cloud (`KAV-99`)

- **Asked:** Roshan asked whether we could build "a container for the cloud": an empty box anyone can put a project into, give a few settings, and have it come up and scale on AWS or another cloud. He wanted a plan and a survey of what exists, with no changes.
- **Researched (2026-10-28):** the box shape already exists as an open standard (Score, CNCF Sandbox), and Radius, Crossplane v2 and kro do the translation, though the last two need Kubernetes. Defang, Encore, HCP Terraform no-code modules and Amazon ECS Express Mode already do "your project into your cloud". The failures teach the most: Wing (a new cloud *language*, $20M) shut down in 2025, and AWS itself retired Proton (2026-10-07) and Copilot CLI (2026-06-12) and closed App Runner to new customers (2026-04-30).
- **Plan agreed:** no new language, no Terraform fork, no Kubernetes needed. Build a small tool on Score's box file, add `size`, `budget` and `cloud` fields, and turn the box into plain OpenTofu. It shows the plan in plain English with its monthly cost and builds only after a human approves. The AI only drafts. Seven gated stages, starting with trying the existing tools (if one fits, use it and stop). Its own repository, **not started until the current Kaval phase closes**.
- **Recorded:** Jira epic `KAV-99` (labels `infra`, `beyond-v1`, the seven stages as acceptance criteria, no stories yet), [future-scope FS-6](../future-scope.md#fs-6--petti-a-box-for-the-cloud), and a line under *Beyond v1* in the roadmap. No ADR: this is a future-scope idea, not a Kaval architecture decision.
- **Found on the way:** the dashboard's Jira links for phases 6, 7 and 8 opened the epic one phase below (6 opened KAV-11, and so on), and Phase 9 opened KAV-14 (*Harden & publish*). Fixed: 6→KAV-12, 7→KAV-13, 8→KAV-14. Phases 5 and 9 both point at KAV-10 for now, because Phase 5's stories (KAV-76..83) were filed under KAV-10, which is titled "Phase 9 — Mobile app", while KAV-11, titled "Phase 5", holds only the older KAV-52..54. Untangling that, and the phase epics still showing *To Do* (KAV-7, 8, 9, 11), is left to Roshan.
- Cost: $0.

## The dashboard shows the Beyond v1 ideas (`KAV-99`)

- **Asked:** Roshan noticed the dashboard had no *Beyond v1* section. KAV-99 appeared only in the commit list and a note, because the dashboard reads just the `## Phase N` headings of the roadmap.
- **Built:** `read_future_scope()` in `scripts/tracking/dashboard.py` reads every `### FS-n` idea in `docs/future-scope.md` and renders a *Beyond v1* section under the phases. Each card shows the summary, a link to the write-up on GitHub, and the epic when the idea has a `Jira epic:` line. The ideas are kept out of the progress numbers. The page stays the only place the ideas are written, so a new FS-7 shows up by itself.
- **Proven:** 13 tests in `test_dashboard_future.py`, including the whole page built from the repo with and without the section (identical progress readout) and the negative cases (no epic gives no Jira link, a missing page gives no section, text after the last idea is not swept in). The first real run gave Petti the card "It would be its own repository.", because the parser took the line under the Jira line. Fixed by skipping the whole Jira paragraph (now tested), and FS-6 gained a one-paragraph summary at the top.
- Cost: $0.
